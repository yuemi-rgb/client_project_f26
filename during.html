During Your Journey

Navigating Your Active Project

Real-world projects rarely follow a perfect plan. Requirements may change, team dynamics can evolve, and clients may face unexpected constraints. During this stage, students learn how to adapt while keeping the project aligned with partner needs.

As the project moves forward, students should regularly communicate with their partners, document their progress, and seek feedback to make sure their work continues to meet project goals. Staying flexible and addressing challenges early can help teams make informed decisions and maintain a productive partnership.

Focus & Objectives

  • Apply human-centered research methods during fieldwork.
  • Keep clients and project sponsors updated on progress.
  • Gather and respond to feedback throughout the project.
  • Manage changing requirements and project challenges.

Helpful Resources

  • AEIOU Observation: Use the AEIOU framework (Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects, and Users) to organize observations during fieldwork. This can help identify user behaviors, needs, and opportunities for improvement.
  • User Needs and Insights: Organize information collected from interviews and observations to identify user needs, common patterns, and actionable insights that can guide project decisions.
  • Feedback Worksheet: Organize mid-project feedback from students, instructors, and community partners. Use the feedback to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments throughout the project.
  • Project Management Worksheet: Break down project goals into manageable tasks, establish milestones, and assign responsibilities to team members. Use the worksheet to track progress and keep the project on schedule.
