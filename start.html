Starting on Your Journey!

Starting the Experience

First impressions shape the trajectory of a partnership. The starting phase is where project ideas turn into structured, professional commitments.

At this stage, students should focus on building a strong relationship with their client or community partner. This includes understanding the partner's needs, discussing realistic project goals, and establishing clear communication expectations. Taking these steps early can help prevent misunderstandings and support effective teamwork throughout the experience.

Focus & Objectives

  • Partner Communication: Establish clear communication channels, discuss expectations, and decide how often to provide updates.
  • First Meetings: Prepare questions about the client's goals, challenges, timeline, and expected project outcomes.
  • Formalizing Agreements: Clarify team responsibilities, project scope, deliverables, and deadlines with the client.

Helpful Tips & Resources

  • Initial Client Email: Introduce your team, briefly explain the project, and suggest a time for the first meeting.
  • First Client Meeting Agenda: Introduce team members, discuss the client's goals and challenges, clarify project expectations, and agree on next steps.
  • Student-Client Partnership: Establish clear expectations about communication, responsibilities, project deliverables, and timelines. Make sure both students and clients understand their roles throughout the project.
  • UMSI Client Guide: Learn about project timelines, client agreements, and expectations for working with a partner.
